Surgical Oncology Domains

Areas of Specialisation

Comprehensive surgical oncology management and evidence-based treatments across specialized solid tumor domains, with an emphasis on organ preservation, robotic precision, and patient quality of life.

Domain 01

Breast Surgical Oncology & Oncoplasty

Specialized surgical treatment for benign and malignant breast conditions. Treatments focus on oncological clearance while maximizing cosmetic outcome and patient self-confidence through oncoplastic remodeling.

Key Surgical Procedures & Interventions:

Breast Conservation Surgery (BCS)
Oncoplastic Reconstruction & Remodeling
Modified Radical Mastectomy (MRM)
Sentinel Lymph Node Biopsy (SLNB)
Axillary Lymph Node Dissection
Triple-Negative & HER2+ Care

Domain 02

Head & Neck Oncology & Thyroid Cancers

Comprehensive management for solid tumors of the oral cavity, tongue, jaw, larynx, pharynx, salivary glands, and thyroid. Procedures prioritize complete oncological clearance alongside preservation of speech, swallowing, and cosmetic contours.

Key Surgical Procedures & Interventions:

Total & Hemithyroidectomy
Central & Lateral Neck Dissections
Oral Cavity & Tongue Tumor Resections
Composite Resections (Commando Surgery)
Parotidectomy & Salivary Gland Tumors
Reconstructive Coordination (Local & Free Flaps)
Domain 03

Gynecological Oncology (Ovarian & Endometrial Cancers)

Specialized surgical treatment for cancers of the female reproductive system, including ovarian, endometrial (uterine), and cervical cancers. Emphasis is placed on radical cytoreduction to achieve microscopic clearance and enhance overall survival.

Key Surgical Procedures & Interventions:

Cytoreductive / Debulking Surgery (Ovarian)
Interval Debulking Surgery (Post-NACT)
Total Hysterectomy & Bilateral Salpingo-Oophorectomy
Radical Hysterectomy (Cervical Malignancies)
Pelvic & Para-Aortic Lymphadenectomy
Fertility-Sparing Surgeries for Early Stages

Domain 04

Musculoskeletal & Soft Tissue Sarcomas

Specialized in limb-sparing oncological resections and management of complex soft tissue and bone sarcomas in extremities, trunk, and retroperitoneum. Supported by Dr. Gayathri's first-prize distinction in the State Ortho-Oncology Quiz (2022).

Key Surgical Procedures & Interventions:

Limb Salvage / Sparing Surgery
Wide Local Excision with 3D Clear Margins
Retroperitoneal Sarcoma Resections
Chest Wall & Abdominal Wall Sarcoma Resections
Compartment Resections
Multidisciplinary Plastic Reconstruction Coordination

Domain 06

Organ Preservation Surgeries

A core philosophy in modern surgical oncology: eradicating cancer thoroughly without needlessly sacrificing healthy surrounding anatomy. Designed to safeguard vital physiological function, appearance, and psychological well-being.

Key Organ-Preserving Approaches:

Breast Conservation Therapy (BCT)
Laryngeal & Voice-Preserving Surgeries
Limb-Sparing Musculoskeletal Resections
Sphincter-Saving Colorectal Procedures
Fertility-Preserving Gynecological Options
Structured Post-Operative Rehabilitation
Domain 07

Gastrointestinal & Abdominal Oncology

Surgical resection of solid malignancies affecting the gastrointestinal tract, stomach, colon, rectum, and abdominal organs. Combines high-precision oncological margin control with advanced anastomotic techniques.

Key Surgical Procedures & Interventions:

Radical Colectomies & Hemicolectomies
Low Anterior Resection (LAR) & Ultra-Low LAR
Gastrectomy (Subtotal & Total with D2 Lymphadenectomy)
Abdominoperineal Resection (APR)
GIST & Small Bowel Tumor Resections
Complex Abdominal Wall Reconstructions

Multidisciplinary Tumor Board (MDT) Care

Every patient benefits from collaborative case evaluation. Dr. Gayathri coordinates in real-time with medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, diagnostic radiologists, and oncopathologists at AIG Hospitals to create a synchronized, personalized road map for care.

Core Pillars of MDT Collaboration:

Synchronized Pre-Operative Staging & Imaging
Neoadjuvant Chemotherapy / Radiation Timing
Individualized Molecular & Genetic Tumor Profiling
Post-Operative Adjuvant Therapy Planning
Palliative Care & Pain Management Integration
Long-Term Surveillance & Survivorship Support
