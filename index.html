Meet Dr. Gayathri Kolagatla

Consultant Surgical Oncologist

Dr. Gayathri Kolagatla is an expert surgical oncologist with advanced specialized training from premier medical institutes like the Nizam's Institute of Medical Sciences (NIMS), Hyderabad. Frequently Asked Questions

A surgical oncologist is a doctor who specializes in using surgery to diagnose, stage, and treat cancer. They work as part of a multidisciplinary team that may include medical oncologists, radiation oncologists, pathologists, and other specialists to create comprehensive treatment plans tailored to each patient.
You may be referred to a surgical oncologist after a diagnosis of cancer, or when a biopsy or imaging reveals a suspicious mass. Early consultation can help determine the most appropriate treatment path and may improve outcomes.
Organ-preservation surgery aims to remove cancerous tissue while keeping the affected organ as functional as possible. Rather than removing an entire organ, surgeons use precise techniques to target only the tumour and necessary margins, helping maintain quality of life.
Minimally invasive techniques, including laparoscopic and robotic-assisted surgery, typically involve smaller incisions. Potential benefits may include reduced blood loss, shorter hospital stays, less post-operative pain, and quicker return to normal activities. Suitability depends on the type, location, and stage of cancer.
A Tumor Board is a conference where specialists from different areas — surgical oncology, medical oncology, radiation oncology, pathology, and radiology — review a patient's case together. This collaborative approach ensures treatment recommendations are comprehensive and incorporate multiple expert perspectives.
